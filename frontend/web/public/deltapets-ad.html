Browser Game Project

DeltaPets

Hatch a Kith, care for it daily, build a party, and battle through a browser-native pet sim built with React, TypeScript, Node, Express, and Supabase.

Hatch

Every Kith can roll its own element, personality, and early stat path.

Care

Daily care keeps your Kith stable through hunger, cleanliness, mood, rest, and comfort systems.

Battle

Train your team, unlock skills, and push toward dungeon-style battle runs.
